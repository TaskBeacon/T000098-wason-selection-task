# Task Plot Brief

Title: Wason Selection Task
Construct: conditional reasoning / falsification

## Rows / representative conditions

- Abstract rule: vowel/even item with visible cards A, D, 4, 7.
- Social contract: car/fuel item with visible cards “借用了汽车”, “没有借汽车”, “加满了油箱”, “没有加满油箱”.

## Participant-visible timeline

- Abstract rule: Fixation (`+`, 500 ms) -> Selection (rule “如果卡片一面是元音字母，那么另一面一定是偶数。”; cards A / D / 4 / 7; press 1–4; up to 30 s per decision) -> Selected set (cards 1 and 4 marked `✓`; press Return to submit; up to 30 s per decision) -> Blank ITI (500 ms).
- Social contract: Fixation (`+`, 500 ms) -> Selection (rule “如果某人借用了我的汽车，那么此人必须把油箱加满。”; cards 借用了汽车 / 没有借汽车 / 加满了油箱 / 没有加满油箱; press 1–4; up to 30 s per decision) -> Selected set (cards 1 and 4 marked `✓`; press Return to submit; up to 30 s per decision) -> Blank ITI (500 ms).

## Visual facts

- Four gray cards appear in one horizontal row beneath the rule.
- The card numbers are 1, 2, 3, 4 from left to right.
- A chosen card gains a `✓` marker; no card is turned over and no correctness feedback appears.
- The figure shows a representative correct P/not-Q selection only to explain interaction flow; the task accepts any submitted subset.
- The blank top 15–18% is reserved for the fixed post-processed header and logo.
